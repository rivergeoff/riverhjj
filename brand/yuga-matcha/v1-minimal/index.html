the idea

01 · concept

a door, a bowl,
and one leaf.

The mark is the café in one shape. The arch is the blue door people look for. Inside it sits a chawan with a wave of whisked foam on top, so it reads as both matcha and the sea. A single tea leaf rises above it like a slow morning sun.

The wordmark is set in a soft, slightly irregular italic serif, so it looks hand-whisked rather than machined. Wide-spaced capitals underneath keep it calm and ordered.
