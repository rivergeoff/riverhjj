the story in the mark

一

every bowl is a
small ceremony.

yuga (悠雅) means graceful, unhurried. The logo is drawn like the drink is made: by hand, one stroke at a time, with nothing hurried.

A single brushed ensō, the Zen circle of one breath, holds a hand-thrown chawan. Its glaze is the sea, with blue seigaiha waves and pale celadon running down over them. Inside is matcha, still foaming from the whisk. Steam rises, a fresh leaf falls, and a worn hanko stamp signs the work like a painter's seal.

The brush stroke isn't perfect, and that's deliberate. The dry, breaking tail shows a person made it.

signed, yuga
