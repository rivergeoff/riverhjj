Symbolism

The Sun

The light of learning. Its rays are drawn as grains of palay, honoring the harvest and the gold of Rosario.

The Book as a Hanging Bridge

An open book forms the deck of a highland hanging bridge strung between the mountains: education connects learners to opportunity and to one another.

The Mountains

Two sunlit peaks and a distant range stand for the upland community the school serves.

The River & Pines

The valley and forest below the bridge: the life of the land the school belongs to.

The Woven Band

A diamond pattern drawn from Cordillera weaving pays tribute to the school’s Indigenous learners and heritage.

The Golden Terraces

Stepped rice terraces in gold: the land, its fields, and its mines, on which the school stands.

  • Below 36 px, use the emblem alone — no wordmark.
  • Clear space on all sides: the width of the sun.
