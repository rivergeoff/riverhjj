Symbolism

Meaning, in numbers

8

Rays of palay

A great sun settling behind the peaks, with the eight rays of the flag’s sun, each drawn as a grain of palay, rising where the flag’s blue and red meet, split as the flag hangs vertically in peacetime (blue left, red right): the light of learning, and the harvest of Rosario.

2

Mountain peaks

A far-flung school in the uplands, and its two departments, elementary and secondary, integrated into one.

1

Book-bridge

An open book is the deck of a highland hanging bridge between the two peaks: education reaches learners even in the mountains and carries them across to opportunity. Together we bridge.

4

Rice terraces

The four DepEd core values, Maka-Diyos, Maka-tao, Makakalikasan and Makabansa, built step by step like terraces in the valley the school serves.

3

Stars

The three stars of the flag: Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.

13

Grains on each stalk

Kindergarten plus Grades 1 to 12: every level of the integrated K–12 school, carried by the palay wreath.

Construction: 8 rays at 19° intervals, symmetric about the vertical axis; stars at 21° intervals; concentric rings at r 128 · 140 · 167 · 197 · 204 · 243.
Anatomy of the Seal

Built like an heirloom

Gold

The gold of Rosario, its mines and its livelihood, in the sun, the wreath, the terraces and every line of the seal.

The woven ring

A Cordillera-style diamond weave encircles the scene: the school’s Indigenous learners and heritage hold everything together.

Rope & ribbon

The motto’s banner rests on a ring of twisted rope, the cable of a highland hanging bridge, holding the whole scene together.

Est. 2012

The founding year at the foot of the outer ring, the way Philippine school seals traditionally carry it.

Logo Family

One seal, three sizes of voice

Typography

Three voices, one hierarchy

Libre Baskerville

Name · Bold

A sturdy, classical serif with clear, open letters. Bold capitals for the school name around the seal; title case in the logos.

Together we bridge

Motto · Italic

The same family’s italic carries the motto on the ribbon and beside the logos: one family, two voices.

MONTSERRAT

Labels · Semibold

A quiet geometric sans, widely spaced, for “Integrated School” and “Est. 2012”.
