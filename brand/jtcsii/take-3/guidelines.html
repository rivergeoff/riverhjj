Symbolism

Meaning, in numbers

13

Rays of palay

Kindergarten plus Grades 1 to 12: every level of the integrated K–12 school rises from the same sun. Drawn as grains of palay for the harvest and the gold of Rosario.

2

Mountain peaks

The elementary and secondary departments: two schools that became one integrated school.

1

Book-bridge

An open book is the deck of a highland hanging bridge between the two peaks. Education joins the levels and carries learners across to opportunity: together we bridge.

4

Rice terraces

The four DepEd core values, Maka-Diyos, Maka-tao, Makakalikasan and Makabansa, built step by step like terraces in the valley the school serves.

3

Stars

Learners, teachers, and parents with the community: the three who succeed together.

13

Grains on each stalk

The palay wreath carries the same thirteen levels as the rays: what the light of learning gives, the community harvests.

Construction: 13 rays at 12.5° intervals across 150°; stars at 21° intervals; concentric rings at r 128 · 140 · 167 · 197 · 204 · 243.
Anatomy of the Seal

Built like an heirloom

The rising sun

A young school, founded 2012, still rising.

The woven ring

A Cordillera-style diamond weave encircles the scene: the school’s Indigenous learners and heritage hold everything together.

The ribbon

The motto, carried across the foot of the seal like a banner.

Est. 2012

The founding year, set at the foot of the outer ring the way Philippine school seals traditionally are.

Logo Family

One seal, three sizes of voice

Typography

Three voices, one hierarchy

Fraunces

Display · Name & motto

A warm, old-style serif with soft, hand-cut details. Used for the school name, headings and the motto in italic.

MARCELLUS

Formal · Seal & descriptor

Flared capitals carved in the tradition of inscriptions. Used for the seal ring, the ribbon and “Integrated School”.

Plus Jakarta Sans

Text · Labels & body

A clear contemporary sans for body copy, labels and small details such as “EST. 2012”.
