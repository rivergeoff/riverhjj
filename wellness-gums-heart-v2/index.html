Your gums & your heart
Bleeding gums. A small thing, until it isn’t.
October 2026
01
What the research says
Your gums and your heart are linked.
A joint report from the European Federation of Periodontology and the World Heart Federation found that gum disease is associated with heart disease.
Sanz et al., 2020, Journal of Clinical Periodontology
02
What it doesn’t say
“A link,
not proof.”
Gum disease hasn’t been shown to cause heart disease, and treating your gums isn’t proven to prevent it.
We’d rather tell you that plainly.
Sanz et al., 2020
