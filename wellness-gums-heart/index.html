The WellnessWhat the research says
Your gums and your heart are linked.
A joint report from the European Federation of Periodontology and the World Heart Federation found that gum disease is associated with heart disease.
Sanz et al., 2020, Journal of Clinical Periodontology02 / 07
The WellnessWhat it doesn't say
A link.
Not proof.
Gum disease hasn’t been shown to cause heart disease, and treating your gums isn’t proven to prevent it. We’d rather tell you that plainly.
Sanz et al., 2020, Journal of Clinical Periodontology03 / 07
