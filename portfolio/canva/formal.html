Project 07 · Case study
Mossline Matcha, a case study.
Spec project · concept brand
The clientA small-batch matcha bar in Davao City selling ceremonial-grade lattes and tins to students and young professionals.
The problemGreat product, forgettable feed: price-list posts, five fonts, no clear voice.
The strategySell the ritual, not the drink — a calm “slow down, sip green” voice on three pillars: ritual, origin, community.
The executionBrand kit, 9-grid feed system, 12 posts, 4 reels and a “first sip” launch campaign with story polls.
Timeline6 weeks
“Nobody follows a café for its menu. They follow it for how it makes their afternoon feel.”
