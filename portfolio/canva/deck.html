Mossline Matcha,a case study.
Client // Mossline Matcha Co.
01 The clientA small-batch matcha bar in Davao City selling ceremonial-grade lattes and tins to students and young professionals.
02 The problemGreat product, forgettable feed: price-list posts, five fonts, no voice. People liked the drinks, not the page.
03 The strategySell the ritual, not the drink — a calm “slow down, sip green” voice built on 3 pillars: ritual, origin, community.
04 The executionNew brand kit, a 9-grid feed system, 12 posts, 4 reels and a “first sip” launch campaign with story polls.
Timeline6 weeks
“Nobody follows a café for its menu. They follow it for how it makes their afternoon feel.”
